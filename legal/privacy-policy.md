**Last updated: [DATE]**

## What we collect
- **Account data:** name/business name, email, phone, postcode, password
  (stored hashed by our authentication provider, never in plain text).
- **Tradie verification data:** ABN, licence number and state, and any
  licence documents you upload.
- **Job content:** job descriptions, photos, quotes, messages between
  Customers and Tradies, and reviews.
- **Payment data:** processed by Stripe, our PCI-compliant payment
  processor. We do not store full card numbers on our servers.
- **Usage data:** device/browser type, IP address, and basic analytics to
  keep the Platform secure and improve it.

## Why we collect it
To operate the marketplace (matching, quoting, messaging, payments), verify
Tradie identity/ABN, prevent fraud and abuse, meet legal obligations (e.g.
tax, AML/CTF obligations that may apply to payment facilitation), and
communicate with you about your account and jobs.

## Who we share it with
- **Other users**, to the extent needed to complete a job (e.g. a Customer
  sees a Tradie's business name, trade, ABN status, and rating; a Tradie
  sees a Customer's job details and approximate location).
- **Service providers**: Supabase (database/authentication/storage), Stripe
  (payments), [ANALYTICS/EMAIL/SMS PROVIDERS], each bound by their own
  privacy and security obligations.
- **Government/regulators** where required by law, or to protect safety.
- We do not sell personal information.
