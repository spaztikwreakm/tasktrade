# Dispute & Refund Policy — TaskTrade AU

**Last updated: [DATE]**

## How payment protection works
When a Customer pays for an accepted quote, funds are **held by the
Platform** (via Stripe) — not paid to the Tradie immediately. They're
released to the Tradie only when:
- the Customer confirms the job is complete and releases payment, or
- [X] days pass after the job is marked "hired" with no dispute raised
  (auto-release — configure this window and state it clearly to users), or
- a dispute is resolved in the Tradie's favour.

## Raising a dispute
If the work isn't done, isn't as agreed, or is unsafe/non-compliant, the
Customer should:
1. First try to resolve it directly with the Tradie via job chat — most
   issues are fixed fastest this way.
2. If unresolved, use "Report this job" before releasing payment, or within
   [X days] of releasing payment for quality issues that emerge later
   (e.g. a leak reappears).

## How we assess a dispute
Our team may request photos, chat history, and both parties' account of
events. We are not a court or a licensed building inspector — for technical
compliance disputes (e.g. "was this wiring done to standard") we may direct
parties to an independent licensed assessor or the relevant state
regulator, and we'll take that outcome into account.

## Possible outcomes
- Full or partial release to the Tradie.
- Full or partial refund to the Customer.
- Funds held further pending independent assessment.
- Referral to the relevant state fair trading body, licensing board, or
  (for amounts within its limit) a state civil/administrative tribunal
  (e.g. NCAT, VCAT, QCAT) — we cannot force either party to accept our
  decision as final if it's a genuine contractual dispute between them,
  since the contract for the work is between Customer and Tradie, not us.

## Chargebacks
If a Customer disputes a charge directly with their bank instead of using
this process, we may suspend the account pending resolution and will supply
transaction/communication records to Stripe as evidence.

## Cancellations
- **Before work starts:** either party can cancel; held funds are refunded
  to the Customer in full, unless the Tradie can show costs already
  reasonably incurred (e.g. materials bought) which may be deducted.
- **After work starts:** handled as a dispute (see above) if either party
  is unhappy with how a cancellation is handled.

## Your consumer rights are not affected
Nothing in this policy limits your rights under the Australian Consumer
Law, including the right to a remedy for services not provided with due
care and skill, or goods supplied as part of a job that aren't of
acceptable quality.
