# Marketplace Rules — TaskTrade AU

These rules sit alongside our [Terms of Service](./terms-of-service.md).
Breaching them can lead to a warning, listing removal, payment hold, or
account suspension.

## For everyone
1. Give accurate information — real name/business name, real ABN, real
   photos of real jobs.
2. Keep all job-related communication respectful. No harassment, threats,
   discrimination, or hate speech.
3. Don't share personal contact details in the first message purely to move
   the deal off-platform and avoid fees — see "Off-platform circumvention"
   below.
4. Don't post the same job/spam multiple times, or use bots/scripts against
   the Platform.

## For Tradies
1. You must hold a valid ABN and, where your trade or state/territory
   requires it, a current licence, registration, or permit before you
   accept a job. Examples where a licence is typically mandatory in most
   states: electrical work, gas fitting, plumbing/drainage, building work
   above a value threshold, and asbestos removal. **Confirm your own state's
   requirements — this list is illustrative, not exhaustive or legal
   advice.**
2. Only quote on jobs within your genuine trade and licence scope.
3. Honour quotes you send; if the scope changes on site, agree any
   variation with the Customer in writing (job chat) before proceeding.
4. Carry appropriate insurance (public liability at minimum) for the work
   you perform.
5. Complete work safely and in line with the relevant Australian Standards
   and state work-health-and-safety law.

## For Customers
1. Describe the job accurately, including access issues and safety hazards.
2. Don't ask Tradies to perform unlicensed work in a licensed trade area.
3. Pay through the Platform so your payment is protected and released only
   once you're satisfied — paying a Tradie directly/outside the Platform
   removes that protection.
4. Leave honest reviews based on the actual job.

## Off-platform circumvention
Asking another user to complete payment outside the Platform to avoid fees
undermines the payment-protection and dispute system for everyone, and is a
breach of these rules for both parties involved.

## Prohibited listings
Illegal work, unlicensed work in a licensed category, unsafe or clearly
non-compliant requests (e.g. bypassing safety switches), and anything
sexual, discriminatory, or unrelated to trade services.

## Reporting
Use the "Report" option on a job or profile, or email [TRUST & SAFETY
EMAIL]. We review reports and may act before responding to the reported
party, to protect user safety.

## Enforcement
We may remove content, hold or reverse a payment pending review, or suspend
an account for a breach of these rules, at our reasonable discretion, with
notice where practical.
